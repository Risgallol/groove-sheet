Pattern grid

Click a cell to cycle: Hit Accent Ghost (snare) Cross-stick (snare) Open (hi-hat) Drag to paint. Right-click or Alt-click a step to split it (e.g. a 16th into two 32nds) or merge it. Click a bar title to select bars for copy and paste; Shift-click selects a range. Click a count (1 e & a) or the score to start playback there. The ↵ button in a bar's header starts a new score line after it.
Saved grooves stay in this browser. Use Share to move a groove to another device or send it to a bandmate. Tempo is always counted in quarter notes, in every time signature.
